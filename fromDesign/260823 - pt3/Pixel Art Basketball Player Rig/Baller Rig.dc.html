THE SPEC
Anchors, slots, and sizing rules. This is the contract every species must satisfy — get it right and the art pipeline never blocks on new aliens.
ANCHOR SKELETON
CROWN — hair / crest / antennae
SKULL — head, eyes, goggles
SHOULDER A/B — arm pairs 1–2
CHEST — jersey + number
HIP — leg row origin, tail
GRIP — ball / brick attach
SOLE — locked to y=46 baseline
Anchors are computed, never authored. Every part declares which anchor it hangs from and its own pivot — that is the whole rig.
